Ever since [the beginning of the year](/blog/2026/01/01/happy_new_year_2026.html), I've been wanting to do a mini reflection on how [the first BlogNov](/blog/2025/11/01/blog_nov_2025.html) went, but never got around to actually writing anything. So, four and a half months later, I guess I'll [just](https://www.todepond.com/wikiblogarden/better-computing/just/) [start writing](/blog/2025/07/05/start_writing.html)!

<!-- excerpt -->

Overall, I think my first attempt at doing a BlogNov went quite well. Even though I stopped writing a little over halfway through the month, I still managed to more than double the number of posts on the blog, [add an RSS feed](/blog/2025/11/12/blog_update_rss_feed.html), and completely rework the last modified dates at the bottom of pages, which I'd been wanting to do for a while.

One of the biggest reasons I lost the motivation to write was that I felt like my posts were getting less and less substantial. Like, rather than having an idea for a post and ruminating on it for a few weeks before getting around to writing for a few days, I had to go from idea to final product within 24 hours. While some posts were good, I felt that most lacked any real thought, and were just a couple paragraphs of me rambling about whatever was on my mind that day. Besides lacking substance, this also got exhausting pretty quickly, so I stopped.

For BlogNov later this year, I'll probably adjust the rules a little to allow myself to write without the requirement of making a finished post every day. In the meantime, I've got a few more posts that have been on the back burner for a few months now, so hopefully they'll see the light of day soon!
